Three ways to ask

  • Capture an area

    Press Alt+S, drag a box around the question, and the answer opens beside it.

  • Highlight text

    Select text on a page, then pick Solve, Explain or Translate.

  • Keep asking

    Type a follow-up, tap Simpler or Why?, or move the answer into Chrome’s side panel.
