Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

ClarityAI is a Chrome extension that answers questions about what you select on a web page. This policy covers the extension, its server and this website: what they collect, why, who else handles it, how long it’s kept and how to delete it.

The short version

  • What you capture or highlight is sent through ClarityAI’s server to the AI model you choose (with Auto, the first of a few models that’s available). The server doesn’t keep screenshots.
  • Your account holds your email address, your name if you give one, and the answers, prompts and settings you save, so they sync between computers.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, from the extension’s Account tab at any time.

What ClarityAI collects

ClarityAI uses this information only to run the extension: to sign you in, answer your questions, sync what you save and prevent abuse.

Your account

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, if you type one when you create an account, or the name on your Google account if you sign in with Google.
  • If you sign in with Google, the ID Google gives your account, so ClarityAI recognises you next time.
  • When the account was created and when it was last used (noted at most once a day).

Signing in

  • Email codes: a keyed hash of each 6-digit code (never the code itself), when it was sent and how many times it was tried. A code works for 10 minutes.
  • Sessions: signing in gives the extension a session token. The server stores only a hash of it and its expiry, 60 days after you last used it.
  • To stop abuse, the server counts the codes requested for each email address and from each IP address, and the codes entered from each IP address, within an hour.
  • To stay within the free plans everyone shares, the server also counts how many requests each AI model and how many sign-in emails are used each day. These counts hold no personal details.

What you save

  • History: for each conversation, the text you highlighted (shortened to 90 characters) or the title of the page you captured from, the answers, your follow-up questions, the model and the action you chose. Your account keeps the latest 50. Screenshots are never saved.
  • Your custom prompts and popup settings: the model, the answer language and the popup’s switches.
  • When you delete an answer from your history, the server keeps only its ID for 90 days, so another computer of yours that still has a copy removes it instead of uploading it again.

Earning answers

  • On the website’s account page you can earn extra answers by watching ads. ClarityAI records each ad view (a random ID, your account, and when it started and finished) for 30 days, and keeps your balance of earned answers until you spend them or delete your account.
  • Earning is in test mode for now, open only to test accounts and with no real ads. Before real ads start, this policy will name the ad network and say what it collects.

Usage

  • How many answers your account asked for on each day, to apply the daily allowance (currently 30, counting follow-ups and Double-checks).

Who at ClarityAI can see your account

  • To run ClarityAI and stop abuse, its owner can see, on an admin page, each account’s email address, name, how it signs in, when it was created and last used, how many answers it used each day, its earned answers, how many devices are signed in, and how many answers, custom prompts and ad views it has. Nobody at ClarityAI reads your saved questions or answers.
  • From that page the owner can change an account’s free answers a day or how many it has left today, give it earned answers or its day’s answers back, sign it out everywhere, block an account that abuses ClarityAI, or delete one. Each of these is recorded (who did it, what changed and to which account) and the record is kept for 180 days.

When you ask a question

Nothing is sent until you start a capture, pick an action for highlighted text, or send a follow-up.

  • When you capture an area, the extension takes a screenshot of the visible part of the tab and crops it to the box you drew. When you highlight text, it uses the text you selected.
  • It sends the cropped image or the text, the instruction (such as Solve, Explain, Translate or your own prompt), the answer language and any follow-up messages to ClarityAI’s server, with your session token.
  • The server checks your session and daily allowance, adds ClarityAI’s key for the provider of the model you picked, forwards the request and streams the answer back. It doesn’t store the image, the text or the answer, apart from your history, described above.
  • With Auto, it goes to Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite, or, when that model is busy or has used up ClarityAI’s free allowance, to the next of Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite, Qwen 3.8 27B (Groq), Nemotron 3.5 Bolt and Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (NVIDIA).
  • Double-check sends the question and the first answer to a second model, from a different company where possible: Nemotron 3.5 Bolt (NVIDIA), Qwen 3.8 27B (Groq) or Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite (Google).

Each provider handles what it receives under its own terms. ClarityAI uses the providers’ free plans. Under Google’s terms for the free Gemini API quota, Google may use what’s sent to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Other providers’ free plans may allow similar use. What stays in your browser

  • Your history, custom prompts and settings are also kept in the extension’s storage in Chrome on your computer. Removing the extension deletes them.
  • Signing out removes your history and custom prompts from this computer; they stay in your account. If someone else then signs in on the same browser, they start with nothing of yours, and nothing of yours is added to their account.
  • Your session token is kept in the extension’s own storage, which web pages can’t read.
  • If you sign in on this website’s account page, that browser keeps your session token in its local storage until you sign out or the session ends. The account page runs only ClarityAI’s own code.
  • To show the highlight button, a small script runs on the pages you visit. It reads text only when you select it, to place the button, and sends nothing until you click Solve, Explain or Translate. You can turn the button off in the popup.
  • ClarityAI doesn’t collect your browsing history, the addresses of the pages you visit, or anything on a page beyond what you capture or highlight and, for captures, the page’s title.

Google sign-in

If you choose Continue with Google, Google asks for your permission and then shares your name, your email address, whether Google has verified that address, and your Google account ID with ClarityAI. ClarityAI asks only for these basic profile details (the openid, email and profile scopes). It gets no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and it uses these details only to create your account, sign you in and show your name in the extension.

ClarityAI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove ClarityAI’s access from your Google Account’s connections page. That doesn’t delete your ClarityAI account: to delete it, use the Account tab (see Your choices).

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare runs ClarityAI’s server, its database and this website. It sees your IP address when you connect, and may keep short-lived logs of requests. Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Brevo sends the sign-in code emails, so it receives your email address and the code. Brevo’s privacy policy.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google.
  • The AI provider of the model you choose, as described above.

ClarityAI doesn’t sell or rent your data, doesn’t use it for advertising, and doesn’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it. Neither the extension nor this website uses cookies, ads, analytics or tracking. If ads are added to the Earn page, this policy will be updated first.

Your choices

  • Delete an answer from your history, or clear it all, in the popup’s History tab. This deletes them from your account too.
  • Export your history as a file from the Account tab.
  • See, change and delete your history, prompts and settings on the website’s account page, from any device.
  • Sign out everywhere, from the account page, ends every session: the extension on every computer and the website on every device.
  • Delete your account from the popup’s Account tab or the account page. This straight away erases your account, history, prompts, settings, earned answers, sessions and answer counts from ClarityAI’s server, and the history on that computer.
  • Sign out at any time from the Account tab.
  • Email 5245363inco@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it deleted. You’ll get a reply within 30 days.

Security

The extension talks to ClarityAI’s server over HTTPS. The AI providers’ keys stay on the server and never reach the extension. Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes, so a copy of the database couldn’t be used to sign in. No system is perfectly secure, but ClarityAI keeps what it holds to the minimum it needs.

Children

ClarityAI isn’t meant for children under 13, and doesn’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email 5245363inco@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Where data is processed

Cloudflare, Brevo, Google and the AI providers run services in many countries, including the United States, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in.
